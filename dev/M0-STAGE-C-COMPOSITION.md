# M0 Stage C: template composition

Date: 2026-09-13.  Base: bfc398b.  This increment lets a family
template group import preceding templates at symbolic universe
arguments.  It supplies a prerequisite for category records at
separate universe pairs.  Stage C and U1 remain open.

## Contract

```text
poly (u, v) group Pair where
specialize Box (u) as Left
specialize Box (v) as Right
def member : ... := ...
end
specialize Pair (0, 1) as P
```

A group starts with at least one specialization of a preceding
family template.  Nonrecursive definitions may follow.  Dependencies
use the group's universe scope, including succ, max and imax.
Ordinary top-level specialization still requires closed levels.
The new reserved word is `group`.

Each dependency contributes renamed families and definitions.
Families check in dependency order.  Imported definitions check in
that order after the families, followed by the group's own members.
Every raw term is traversed and kernel checked under the group's
scope.  A callback supplies syntax, never a certified global.
Failures and budget exhaustion prevent later callbacks and discard
the temporary environment.  Only the completed group enters the
catalog.

Specialization prefixes every imported family and definition, then
rechecks the result against the caller's current globals.  In the
example, the families are P_Left and P_Right.  A member named value
in Box becomes P_Left_value and P_Right_value.  The group's own
member becomes P_member.  P is a prefix, not an ordinary global or
a family.  A nested dependency keeps every prefix in its path.
Constructor labels keep their existing names and nominal families
remain distinct, including repeated imports at equal levels.

Unknown or forward dependencies, wrong universe arities, escaping
levels, duplicate prefixes, name collisions, axioms and recursive
members are refused.  A dependency prefix cannot reuse family or
definition names generated by earlier dependencies, as with explicit
specialization.  Existing constructor-label and template-name
reservations apply to the imported names and the closed instance.
A group can import another completed group.  Declaration order
prevents cyclic dependencies.

## Validation

The TEMPLATE-COMPOSITION gate exercises source round trips, nested
prefixes, mixed universe substitutions, member computation, nominal
separation, exact refusals, closed rechecking and cancellation.
The cancellation cases refuse at the dependency fold poll and at the
member poll, and no later callback runs.  The dependency case gives
one unknown dependency and a poll that refuses on its second call.
Only the poll inside the dependency fold can then refuse, because a
checker without that poll finds the unknown schema first and gives a
different refusal.
It also checks a mixed-universe functor shape using the category
portion of the current MechCategory source.  Runtime validation
compares the kernel, Node and Wasmtime at two payloads.  The nested
export takes its type from the group body member project, so a change
of that member stops the kernel check of the export.  That member
gives no host value, because its universe argument is erased.
Mutation controls require compiled mutants, their named failures,
and a passing restored suite.  Measurements are recorded in
dev/M0-BUILD-LOG.md and dev/validation/stage-c-composition/.

## Remaining work

Composition imports family templates and their members.  Standalone
definition templates, local family declarations, parameterized
imports of existing nominal instances and selective imports remain
outside this increment.  Sharing one nominal family between repeated
dependencies also stays outside this increment.
The heterogeneous category prelude still needs its public
functor, natural-transformation and left Kan APIs and source-type
parity.  The M0 mapping gate remains due.

Kernel rules, erasure, encoder and vendor sources keep their bytes.
No axiom, mapping verdict, denominator, trusted-line bound or gate
watchdog changes.
